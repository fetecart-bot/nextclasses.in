Practical learning for a changing world

What students can learn

AI and automation

Practical learning paths covering generative AI tools, AI agents, automation, coding and AI-assisted media creation.

Languages and communication

Programs for spoken English, French, German, public speaking and articulation.

Entrance preparation

Preparation paths for NEET, JEE, KEAM, AISSEE Sainik School and Navodaya examinations, including practice materials and mock tests.

How learning works

  1. Choose a course from the official NextClasses.in catalogue and review its syllabus, level, language and price.
  2. Complete enrollment on the secure checkout.
  3. Use the private student portal to access the assigned course and study materials.
  4. Continue at your own pace and use the portal’s course-aware learning support for questions.
