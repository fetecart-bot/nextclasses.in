## Weekly operating dashboard
Count separately: landing visits by source; course views; trial/enquiry starts; completed registrations; first lesson opens; quiz completions; active trial households; actual verified purchases; refunds/cancellations; AI usage cost.
Compute: registration conversion = completed registrations / landing visits; activation = first-lesson users / registered users; trial-to-paid = purchasers / trials that have reached a purchase decision; acquisition cost = attributed ad spend / new paying households; mentor cost = actual API spend per active learner.
Do not label clicks as unique people or leads as purchases. Use aggregate funnel events without child names, messages or phone numbers in analytics. Validate event delivery before relying on the dashboard.

## Spending and decision rules
No additional ad budget authorized by this plan. When approved, test one audience, one offer and one landing page per campaign. Evaluate on registrations, activation and paid customers—not followers or cheap clicks alone. Pause a paid campaign if links break, trial admission fails or attribution is missing. Set a spend cap before starting; avoid changing multiple variables at once. Review a complete cohort before claiming the 30-day trial has failed or succeeded.

## Honest launch checklist
Three clear paths; correct catalogue on cached devices; retired products rejected by checkout; existing purchased access intact; exact monthly terms; trial quota/expiry; guardian/privacy handling; functioning demo; real support; monitored payment errors; rollback available. Publish only functionality that exists.
